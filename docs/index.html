About

A directory for the whole org fair.

Campus Compass gathers every accredited student organization at Xavier University – Ateneo de Cagayan into one page you can search, filter and carry in your pocket on the day.

What you can do here

  • Browse all 69 organizations across the eleven Xavier Ateneo clusters, one cluster at a time.
  • Search by interest — type coders, farm, music or volunteer and get the orgs that actually match.
  • Open any org for what they do, how to reach them, and their Facebook page.
  • Find their tent on the fair map, with the spot lit and a landmark to walk toward.
